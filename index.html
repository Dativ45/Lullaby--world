The way we listen

Care is part of
the collection.

01

Respect the source

Every recording needs clear provenance, attribution and rights review before publication.

02

Protect young listeners

No public chat, comments, direct messages or unnecessary personal accounts.

03

No endless feed

No autoplay loops designed to keep children listening. Families stay in control.

04

Culture with context

We aim to credit communities and explain traditions without flattening their differences.

For the songs that stay with us.
